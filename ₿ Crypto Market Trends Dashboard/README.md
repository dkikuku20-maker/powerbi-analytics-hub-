# ₿ Crypto Market Trends Dashboard

An interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze cryptocurrency market trends, trading activity, volatility behavior, and comparative crypto performance using historical digital asset market data. 

---

# 📊 Project Overview

This project transforms cryptocurrency market data into actionable insights for investors, analysts, and financial decision-makers.

The dashboard focuses on:

* Market capitalization analysis
* Trading volume trends
* Cryptocurrency price movement
* Market volatility behavior
* Comparative asset performance
* Investor sentiment and risk analysis

---

# 🚀 Dashboard Pages

## 📌 Page 1 — Crypto Market Trends Overview

Provides a high-level overview of cryptocurrency market performance and trading activity.

### Dashboard Includes

* Total Market Cap
* Average Close Price
* Total Trading Volume
* Number of Coins
* Price Trends Over Time
* Market Share by Cryptocurrency
* Trading Volume by Cryptocurrency

### Key Insights

* Bitcoin dominated market capitalization with over **70% market share**
* Ethereum maintained the second-largest market presence
* Trading activity remained heavily concentrated among top cryptocurrencies
* Price trends displayed strong volatility across time periods
* Market performance was highly influenced by Bitcoin price movement

---

## 📌 Page 2 — Crypto Market Comparison

Focused on comparative analysis between major cryptocurrencies.

### Analysis Includes

* Total Trading Volume by Cryptocurrency
* Total Market Cap by Cryptocurrency
* Comparative Price and Volume Analysis
* Cryptocurrency Selection Filtering

### Key Insights

* Bitcoin led significantly in both market cap and trading volume
* Ethereum maintained strong secondary market positioning
* Smaller cryptocurrencies showed substantially lower liquidity
* Market valuation gaps highlighted concentration risk within major assets
* Top cryptocurrencies controlled most market activity

---

## 📌 Page 3 — Crypto Volatility & Market Behavior

Focused on market volatility patterns and investor behavior analysis.

### Analysis Includes

* Volatility Trends Over Time
* Average Volatility by Cryptocurrency
* Trading Volume vs Price Relationship
* Cryptocurrency Volatility Comparison

### Key Insights

* Dogecoin exhibited the highest average volatility among analyzed assets
* Bitcoin showed comparatively lower volatility despite market dominance
* Volatility spikes often aligned with significant price movements
* Trading volume strongly influenced short-term market behavior
* Investor sentiment appeared highly reactive to market fluctuations

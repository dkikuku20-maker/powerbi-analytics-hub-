# 🏥 Healthcare Operations Analytics Dashboard

An interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze healthcare operations, patient demographics, provider performance, and financial trends using real-world healthcare data. 

---

# 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw healthcare operational data into actionable business insights for hospitals, healthcare administrators, and decision-makers.

The dashboard provides visibility into:

* Patient activity and demographics
* Medical expenses and insurance coverage
* Provider and specialty performance
* Hospital operational trends
* Financial and healthcare utilization insights

---

# 🚀 Dashboard Pages

## 📌 Page 1 — Healthcare Operations Overview

High-level operational and financial KPIs including:

* Total Procedures
* Total Medical Expenses
* Average Insurance Payment
* Patient Distribution by Gender
* Medical Expenses by Insurance Provider
* Patient Volume by State
* Top Diagnosis Groups by Expenses

### Key Insights

* Total medical expenses exceeded **$12.9M**
* Medicare and Commercial insurance contributed the largest share of healthcare spending
* Female patients represented the majority of patient activity
* California and Texas recorded the highest patient volume
* Certain diagnosis groups significantly increased operational healthcare costs

---

## 📌 Page 2 — Provider & Financial Performance Analysis

Focused on provider operations and financial efficiency.

### Analysis Includes

* Top providers by medical expenses
* Insurance vs patient payment comparison
* Provider specialty distribution
* Hospital procedure volume
* Medical expenses by specialty

### Key Insights

* Internal Medicine generated the highest operational expenses
* Insurance providers covered most healthcare costs compared to direct patient payments
* Hospital procedure volumes varied significantly across facilities
* A small group of providers contributed heavily to total medical expenses

---

## 📌 Page 3 — Patient & Demographic Insights

Focused on demographic and regional healthcare utilization.

### Analysis Includes

* Patient payments by state
* Healthcare expenses by gender
* Patient age distribution
* Top cities by patient volume
* Regional patient concentration

### Key Insights

* California, Texas, and Florida showed the highest patient engagement
* Patient age distribution was relatively balanced across age groups
* Female patients generated slightly higher healthcare expenses
* Major cities concentrated a significant share of healthcare utilization

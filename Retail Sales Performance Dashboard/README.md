# 🛒 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, profitability trends, customer behavior, and operational efficiency using Superstore sales data. 

---

# 📊 Project Overview

This project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business intelligence insights to support strategic decision-making across sales, operations, and profitability management.

The dashboard focuses on:

* Revenue and profit analysis
* Product category performance
* Customer segment behavior
* Regional sales trends
* Operational shipping insights
* Profitability and risk analysis

---

# 🚀 Dashboard Pages

## 📌 Page 1 — Retail Sales Performance Overview

Provides a high-level overview of sales, profitability, and category performance.

### Dashboard Includes

* Total Revenue
* Profit Margin
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit by Category
* Sales Trend Over Time
* Sales by Category

### Key Insights

* Total revenue exceeded **$1.57M**
* Technology generated the strongest profitability performance
* Office Supplies contributed the highest revenue volume
* Furniture produced lower profit relative to revenue
* Revenue trends remained relatively stable over time

---

## 📌 Page 2 — Profitability & Risk Analysis

Focused on understanding profit drivers and operational risk areas.

### Analysis Includes

* Total Profit by Sub-Category
* Profit by Customer Segment
* Sales vs Profit Analysis
* Profit by Region

### Key Insights

* Consumer segment generated the highest profitability
* Profitability was concentrated in a few high-performing sub-categories
* Some products generated strong sales but weak profit margins
* The West region significantly outperformed other regions in total profit
* Regional disparities revealed opportunities for operational optimization

---

## 📌 Page 3 — Customer & Operational Insights

Focused on customer purchasing behavior and operational performance.

### Analysis Includes

* Revenue by Customer Segment
* Sales by Shipping Mode
* Revenue by Region
* Customer & Operational Insights Summary

### Key Insights

* Standard Class shipping dominated operational sales volume
* Customers showed strong preference for lower-cost shipping options
* Revenue concentration remained strongest in the West region
* The South region underperformed compared to other regions
* Customer behavior patterns revealed opportunities for targeted growth strategies
